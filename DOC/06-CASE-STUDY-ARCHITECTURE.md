# ARTIT.hu — Case Study Architecture

## Purpose

Case studies are evidence, not portfolio decoration.

Narrative:

> business context → problem → why standard software was insufficient → solution logic → practical system → result → long-term evolution

## Shared structure

### 01 — Hero

Story-driven title, not technology title.

Metadata may include:

- industry;
- type;
- lifecycle;
- status.

### 02 — Starting situation

Explain how the business operated and what needed support.

### 03 — Why packaged software was not enough

Use only when supported by project facts.

### 04 — Solution logic

Explain the operating model before listing features.

### 05 — How it works in practice

Use real screenshots with business context.

### 06 — Integrations and environment

Only where relevant.

Explain business meaning, not only technical interfaces.

### 07 — Business result

Use verified outcomes only.

No invented ROI or percentages.

### 08 — Long term

Explain evolution over years:

- changing processes;
- maintenance;
- integration changes;
- technology/environment changes;
- continued use.

### 09 — What we learned

Optional but valuable first-hand expertise.

### 10 — Related competency

Link to:

- business applications;
- integration.

### 11 — CTA

> Van hasonló problémája?

## Fact status

"Known" below reflects internal working understanding used to shape the narrative, not confirmed publication-ready fact. Every publication-facing claim remains gated by the "TO VERIFY" list for its case study and by `13-CONTENT-GAPS-AND-VALIDATION.md`.

## Case Study 01

Working title:

> Egy üzleti rendszer, amely együtt nőtt a vállalkozással.

Known:

- climate/HVAC service and project company;
- approx. 20–50 people;
- system used since company start;
- approx. 10 years / 10+ years direction;
- still in daily use;
- offer management;
- tickets;
- work sheets/orders;
- approval process;
- mobile work sheet;
- related operational functions;
- considered a professional predecessor to Tardify.

TO VERIFY:

- exact launch year;
- exact lifecycle wording;
- exact workflow;
- exact company size at publication;
- customer name/logo permission;
- screenshot publication/anonymization;
- Tardify origin-story wording.

## Case Study 02

Working title:

> A rendszer, amely több ERP-t is túlélt.

Known:

- building services / HVAC company;
- approx. 30–200 people;
- multiple departments;
- custom internal intranet system;
- work-number management;
- partner management;
- internal business processes;
- ERP integration;
- 20+ year direction;
- survived multiple surrounding ERP/business-system generations.

TO VERIFY:

- exact launch year;
- exact system age;
- exact ERP timeline;
- which ERP names may be published;
- integration architecture at a publishable level;
- customer name/logo permission;
- screenshots;
- why the system remained valuable for 20+ years.

## Visual language

Use:

- real screenshots;
- engineering metadata;
- timelines;
- large evidence statements.

Do not:

- redesign legacy UI to look modern;
- hide age;
- use fake device mockups;
- turn the story into a tech-stack showcase.
