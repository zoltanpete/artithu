# ARTIT.hu — Project Overview

## Purpose

Create a new ARTIT.hu marketing website as a greenfield Astro project.

This is not a WordPress redesign or migration. The previous WordPress implementation is being retired.

The site must position ARTIT clearly as a senior custom business software development partner for companies whose processes are too specific for spreadsheets or compromise-heavy off-the-shelf software.

## Business priorities

1. Tardify growth is the primary business focus.
2. ARTIT seeks a small number of high-value custom software projects.

## Primary audience

Typically 5–50 person SMEs with established business processes that are no longer supported well by their current tools.

Common symptoms:

- business-critical Excel sheets;
- manual copying between systems;
- multiple disconnected tools;
- undocumented process knowledge held by one person;
- off-the-shelf software that forces operational compromises;
- systems that work independently but do not communicate.

## Core positioning

> Egyedi üzleti szoftverfejlesztés senior tapasztalattal.

Core message direction:

> Amikor a kész szoftver már nem elég.

> A szoftver alkalmazkodjon a vállalkozásához. Ne fordítva.

Key strategic principle:

> Nem minden problémára kell egyedi szoftver. Amire viszont igen, arra pontosan illeszkedőt építünk.

## Main competencies

- Egyedi üzleti alkalmazások
- Folyamatdigitalizálás
- Rendszerintegráció és automatizáció

Information architecture decision:

`Folyamatdigitalizálás` is a horizontal competency, not a separate landing page in v1.

## Differentiators

- business problem first, technology second;
- senior direct collaboration;
- long-lived production systems;
- own SaaS product: Tardify;
- real software and real project evidence rather than generic agency claims.

## Design direction

Internal concept:

> Software built to last.

Visual character:

- restrained;
- editorial;
- engineering;
- typography-led;
- rigorous grid;
- generous whitespace;
- real software interfaces;
- subtle engineering metadata;
- minimal decorative UI.

## Technical direction

Preferred stack:

- Astro;
- TypeScript;
- static-first;
- content-driven architecture;
- minimal client JavaScript;
- Astro image pipeline;
- semantic HTML;
- accessibility and performance as first-class requirements.

## Decisions that should not be reopened without evidence

- ARTIT is not positioned as a general web/IT agency.
- Primary target is typically 5–50 person SMEs.
- Tardify remains a separate brand and domain.
- ARTIT focuses on custom business applications, process digitalization, integration and automation.
- Business process understanding comes before technology selection.
- Long-lived systems are a primary proof point.
- ARTIT should not visually clone Tardify.
- Astro is the preferred technical foundation.
- Content and IA precede visual implementation.
